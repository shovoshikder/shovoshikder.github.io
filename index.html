About

Shovo Shikder (Bengali: শুভ শিকদার) is a social media cybersecurity specialist and digital rights advocate focused on account security, digital content protection, and community training. He also serves as a trainer and administrator for Ummah Cyber Force Bangladesh.

Areas of Focus

  • Social media account security and hacked/disabled account recovery
  • Digital content protection, DMCA compliance, and anti-impersonation
  • Cybersecurity community training and administration (Ummah Cyber Force Bangladesh)
  • Freelance translation (English, Spanish, and French)
